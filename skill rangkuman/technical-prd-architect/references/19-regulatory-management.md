# Regulatory management

## Review guidance
Record rule maker, edition/version/year, jurisdiction/competition, source URL, applicability, assumptions, implementation parameter, owner and verification date. Never invent SEM/KMHE limits or presume old rules apply now. Regulatory presets must be independently versioned.

## Output contract
Record confirmed evidence, any proposed additions, unresolved TBDs, requirement IDs and the verification method. Distinguish direct source facts from recommendations.
