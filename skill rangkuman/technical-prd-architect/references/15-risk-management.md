# Risk management

## Review guidance
Risk register with ID, cause, consequence, likelihood, impact, detectability where meaningful, mitigation, owner, verification and residual risk. Distinguish project risk, technical risk, privacy/security and physical safety hazards. Define stop/go criteria for high severity.

## Output contract
Record confirmed evidence, any proposed additions, unresolved TBDs, requirement IDs and the verification method. Distinguish direct source facts from recommendations.
