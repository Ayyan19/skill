# Methodology and reproducibility

## Audit checklist
- Research question, hypotheses and theory fit design.
- Population, sample selection, inclusion/exclusion, confounding and generalizability.
- Unit of analysis and independence; repeated measures, clustering, longitudinal structure.
- Experimental controls, randomization/blinding where relevant, baseline selection and ablations.
- Pre-registration/protocol deviation if relevant, ethics approval and consent as required.
- Data acquisition device/calibration, units, preprocessing and operational definitions.
- Sample size justification, missingness, outliers, exclusions and sensitivity.
- Reproducible software versions, code, seeds, split definitions, data provenance and licenses.
- Limitations, alternative explanations and uncertainty of inference.

## Reporting standard
Choose reporting guideline by design and field (e.g. PRISMA for systematic review, CONSORT for trials, STROBE for observational designs, relevant AI/ML standards). Verify latest applicable edition. EQUATOR distinguishes reporting guidance from study-quality assessment; a complete checklist does not prove a good study.
