# Example: DOI audit
Step 1 read reference string. Step 2 verify DOI resolution and publisher/Crossref/DataCite metadata. Step 3 compare title/authors/venue/year. Step 4 open the paper or accessible full text and check specific cited claim. Step 5 log bibliography and claim support separately. An inaccessible full text is not evidence that a claim is false; mark support unverified.
